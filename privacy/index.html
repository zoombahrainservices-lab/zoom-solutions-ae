Privacy policy

This page describes what zoomsolutions.ae collects. It was last updated on 6 October 2026.

The site is published for Zoom Solutions FZC in Sharjah and Zoom Solutions W.L.L. in Bahrain (commercial registration 68542-05).

Quote form

The quote form asks for your name, email, the service, and a description of the shipment. Company, phone, origin, destination, shipment type, temperature range and dates are included when you fill them in. A hidden field is used to ignore automated submissions.

Submit sends that information by email to ebrahim@zoomsolutions.ae and copies neaz@zoombahrain.co. Delivery uses FormSubmit, which receives the message in order to pass it on. The offices keep the correspondence for as long as the enquiry needs.

Email and WhatsApp

Email links open your own mail app. WhatsApp links open WhatsApp. Those services then handle the conversation under their own terms.

Measurement

The site uses Google Analytics 4, measurement ID G-KWK96N34RF, with IP anonymisation. It records page views and these events: quote submitted, WhatsApp click, email click and phone click. The same measurement ID is used on zoombahrain.co, so reports can be filtered by the hostname zoomsolutions.ae. Zoom does not sell personal data.

Hosting

The pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. That host may keep ordinary access logs. Zoom does not operate a separate account database on this site.

Questions

Write to ebrahim@zoomsolutions.ae or neaz@zoombahrain.co.
