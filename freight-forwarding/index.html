Freight forwarding

Air, sea and road freight from Bahrain.

Zoom Solutions W.L.L. coordinates commercial cargo by air, sea and road. Each instruction is built from the origin, destination, cargo, timing and whether pickup and delivery are required. Zoom coordinates the movement. It does not operate aircraft, vessels, ports or a published truck fleet.

What can be coordinated

One freight instruction can cover the mode, the documents and the delivery.

Air freight

Priority, express and other time-sensitive cargo moving by air into or out of Bahrain, including import and export. Door-to-door support is included when pickup and delivery are part of the instruction.

Sea freight

FCL when the cargo needs a full container. LCL when a smaller shipment moves as consolidated cargo. Bulk sea freight can also be coordinated. Zoom does not operate ships or ports.

Road freight

Full truckload and partial loads inside Bahrain, and road freight between Bahrain and Saudi Arabia via the King Fahad Causeway when that corridor is instructed. Wider GCC freight is confirmed against the shipment. Zoom does not keep local offices in those other markets.

Customs support

Import and export documents, customs-file support and customs brokerage coordination. Classification, duties and release are decided by the authority, not published here as fixed rates or times.

Door-to-door or port-to-port

Door-to-door freight is coordinated when pickup and final delivery are in the instruction. Port-to-port movement is also available.

Order fulfilment

Where the instruction needs it, Zoom can coordinate order processing, inventory, pick and pack, packaging support and last-mile delivery. Capacity is confirmed against the order profile.

Temperature-sensitive healthcare cargo is planned on the cold chain logistics page. Equipment and the temperature range are confirmed for that shipment. Zoom does not publish an owned reefer fleet.

How a quote is built

Send the cargo facts. The route is confirmed against them.

01

Define the shipment

Origin, destination, cargo description, weight, dimensions or volume, packages or pallets, preferred mode, pickup, delivery and timing.

02

Confirm the route

Zoom reviews the requirement and coordinates the air, sea or road route. Carrier and transit time are confirmed for that file.

03

Move the file

Documents and any customs coordination are organised, and Zoom follows the file through the instructed stage. This is not a public tracking portal.
