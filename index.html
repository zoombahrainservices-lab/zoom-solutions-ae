Controlled shipment process

From pickup to final delivery, every step protects product integrity.

Our shipment workflow supports sensitive healthcare cargo from planning and collection to packing, monitoring and proof of delivery.

See How Shipments Are Handled
  1. 01
    Shipment review

    Product type, temperature range, destination, documents and urgency are reviewed.

  2. 02
    Packaging preparation

    Suitable passive packaging is selected and prepared according to shipment requirements.

  3. 03
    Validated pickup

    Samples are collected using trained drivers and temperature-controlled handling.

  4. 04
    Quality check and sealing

    Temperature is confirmed before packing, sealing and dispatch.

  5. 05
    Live monitoring

    Temperature, light and location can be tracked until delivery.

  6. 06
    Final delivery

    Care team coordination and proof of delivery complete the shipment cycle.
