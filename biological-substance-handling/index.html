What is biological substance handling?

Biological substance handling is the controlled logistics process for moving biological samples, clinical materials and other sensitive healthcare substances using suitable packaging, documentation, monitoring and delivery coordination.

Do you support cell and gene therapy shipments?

Yes. Zoom Solutions supports temperature-sensitive biological shipments including cell and gene therapy materials, depending on route, temperature range and documentation requirements.

Can you arrange medical express service?

Yes. Depending on the shipment requirement, Zoom Solutions can support medical express options for urgent biological sample movements.

Can biological shipments be tracked live?

Yes. Shipments can be monitored for temperature, light and location until delivery, with care team support during transit.
