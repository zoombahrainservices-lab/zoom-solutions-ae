Who we are

A healthcare logistics partner for sensitive supply chains.

Zoom Solutions provides professional and tailor-made supply chain solutions for healthcare customers that need speed, control, visibility and care throughout the shipment lifecycle.

Our work focuses on pharmaceutical cold chain logistics, biological substance handling, temperature-controlled packaging, sample collection support, air cargo, road freight and real-time monitoring.

We support customers with dedicated care, operational planning and controlled processes designed to help sensitive shipments arrive safely, on time and at the required temperature.
